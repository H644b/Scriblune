# scriblune

scriblune is a shared study desk for the messy middle of learning.

it gives students a place to bring an assignment, a worksheet, a sketch, or
an unfinished idea and work through it with an ai tutor. the tutor can read
the page, explain the next step, draw alongside the student, and change
approaches when the first explanation does not click.

the point is not to hand over an answer. scriblune is built around the moment
understanding starts to take shape: asking a focused question, pointing to the
exact part that feels confusing, trying an idea, and keeping the student's own
thinking visible.

students can use shared ink, page-aware questions, hints, memory pins, scratch
work, and review tools across subjects such as math, science, reading, and
writing. their work remains theirs, their pace sets the rhythm, and a finished
submission stays separate from the teaching that helped create it.

scriblune is for learners who are stuck, curious, or somewhere in between.
less “i’m stuck.” more “i get it.”
